📖 Come, Follow Me · Lesson 48 · Nov 23–29

“Seek the Lord, and Ye Shall Live”

📚 Amos; Obadiah; Jonah
Jonah sailed away from the job God gave him, and a great storm followed. But the Lord hadn’t given up on Jonah, on the people of Nineveh, or on any of us. He keeps sending prophets with this message: if we repent, we will not be cast off forever, because of Jesus Christ it’s not too late to change.

💡 Main principles

📣
The Lord reveals truth through His prophets.Amos 3:1–8; 7:10–15Just like a roaring lion means there is prey nearby, a prophet speaking means God has spoken. Having prophets is a way God shows He loves us and tells us what we need to know.
🌱
“Seek the Lord, and ye shall live.”Amos 4:6–13; 5:4, 14–15When hard things happen, God hopes we will turn to Him. Looking for Him in our daily lives brings real joy and life.
🍞
The word of the Lord can satisfy spiritual hunger and thirst.Amos 8:11–12Without God’s word, we’d feel spiritually hungry, like having no food. The Restoration of the gospel ended that famine, and reading the scriptures fills us up.
🏛️
Who are the “saviours … on mount Zion”?Obadiah 1:21When we do temple work for people who have died, we help them in a small way like the Savior helps us.
🐋
The Lord is merciful to all who turn to Him.Jonah 1–4Jonah ran away, was swallowed by a great fish, and then obeyed. The people of Nineveh turned to God and He forgave them. Jonah was upset, but God wanted him to learn mercy too.

🗣️ Family discussion

  1. What conclusions can we draw when a prophet prophesies? What else do you learn about prophets from Amos 7:10–15?
  2. What has the Lord revealed to you through His prophets?
  3. How has seeking the Lord brought you life? (Amos 5:4, 14–15)
  4. How do you know when you are spiritually hungry? (Amos 8:11–12)
  5. What do you learn from Jonah that can help you be more merciful?

📜 Key verses

Surely the Lord God will do nothing, but he revealeth his secret unto his servants the prophets.Amos 3:7
For thus saith the Lord unto the house of Israel, Seek ye me, and ye shall live:Amos 5:4
Behold, the days come, saith the Lord God, that I will send a famine in the land, not a famine of bread, nor a thirst for water, but of hearing the words of the Lord:Amos 8:11
And God saw their works, that they turned from their evil way; and God repented of the evil, that he had said that he would do unto them; and he did it not.Jonah 3:10
And he prayed unto the Lord, and said, I pray thee, O Lord, was not this my saying, when I was yet in my country? Therefore I fled before unto Tarshish: for I knew that thou art a gracious God, and merciful, slow to anger, and of great kindness, and repentest thee of the evil.Jonah 4:2

🎙️ Prophet's words

having prophets is a sign of God’s loveElder Ulisses Soares, “Prophets Speak by the Power of the Holy Spirit,” Ensign or Liahona, May 2018, 99

🎨 Try this together

Prophets are messengersWhisper a simple message to one person and ask them to pass it to the others. How is the messenger like a prophet? Why does the Lord give us prophets? Then sing “Follow the Prophet” (Children’s Songbook, 110–11), and share why you choose to follow God’s prophet. Bonus: write out Amos 3:7, then cover a few words at a time until everyone can say it from memory.

🛡️ For the Strength of Youth · November chapter

11. Truth will make you free

John 8:32
Your Heavenly Father is a God of truth. All truth comes from Him and leads to Him. You show that you value truth when you keep learning, live with integrity, and bravely stand for what you know is right.

💡 Main teachings

📖
Always be learningHeavenly Father wants His children to keep learning—at school, in training, and from sources you trust. Education isn’t just about money; it’s part of becoming more like Him. Ask the Lord to guide you.
✝️
Learn spiritual truthsWhile you learn about the world, learn about the Savior who created it. Seminary and personal gospel study help you know Him and prepare to serve a mission and share the gospel.
🤝
Live with integrityIntegrity means loving what is right more than being comfortable or popular. Don’t steal, lie, cheat, or deceive—at school, at work, or online. Be the same person when no one is watching.
🎁
You have something precious to shareThe gospel holds answers to life’s questions. You don’t have to know everything—you know enough to love your neighbor, share true principles, and invite friends to learn more.
❓
Questions can build faithHaving questions isn’t weakness. Joseph Smith was 14 when he asked a question with faith. Look for answers in the scriptures, the prophets, family, leaders, and God—and keep living what you already know.

📜 Chapter scripture

And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free.John 8:32

⭐ Key quotes

Living with integrity means you love what is right in the sight of God—more than you love personal comfort, popularity, or convenience. It means doing what is right simply because it is right.For the Strength of Youth
Be the same faithful follower of Jesus Christ in public and in private.For the Strength of Youth
Honesty brings peace and self-respect.For the Strength of Youth
When you stand up for the teachings of Jesus Christ, He stands with you. Jesus Christ is your strength!For the Strength of Youth

🎁 Promised blessing

Education increases your ability to serve the Lord, including as a missionary. It empowers you to bless others, especially your family. The more you learn, the more you can help build God’s kingdom and influence the world for good.

🤔 Questions to ponder

  1. Why does God want you to learn about Him and the world around you?
  2. What should you do when you have questions?
  3. Do you strive to be honest in all that you do?
  4. Am I the same follower of Jesus Christ online and in private as I am at church?
  5. How can I stand up for what is right without being unkind to people who believe differently?
