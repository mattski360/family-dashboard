“Before I Formed Thee in the Belly I Knew Thee”
Jeremiah 1–3; 7; 16–18; 20
Prayer: TannerLukeWyatt
Today Jeremiah 1:5
“Before I formed thee in the belly I knew thee”
Heavenly Father knew each of us before we were born and has work for us to do. What strength has He given you?
Today Jeremiah 1:6–8
“Be not afraid of their faces: for I am with thee”
Jeremiah felt too young, but the Lord promised to be with him. What hard thing can you try today with His help?
Today Jeremiah 2:13
“they have forsaken me the fountain of living waters”
Jesus is a fountain that never runs dry. How can we drink from His living water today?
Today Jeremiah 3:14–15
“I will take you one of a city, and two of a family, and I will bring you to Zion”
The Lord gathers His children one by one. Who can we include or invite this week?
Today Jeremiah 16:16
“Behold, I will send for many fishers, saith the Lord”
President Nelson compared these fishers and hunters to missionaries. How can we help gather Israel?
Today Jeremiah 18:6
“as the clay is in the potter’s hand, so are ye in mine hand”
The Lord shapes us like a potter shapes clay. What can we let Him help us change?
Today Jeremiah 1:9
“Behold, I have put my words in thy mouth”
Prophets speak the Lord’s words. Share one thing you remember from the prophet at general conference.
What did Jeremiah learn about himself in Jeremiah 1:5, and how is it true for you too?
