Come, Follow Me · Oct 12–18
“The Redeemer Shall Come to Zion”
Isaiah 58–66
Prayer: TannerLukeWyatt
Today Isaiah 58:6–7
“Is not this the fast that I have chosen? … to deal thy bread to the hungry”
Fasting isn’t just going without — it lifts heavy burdens and feeds people in need. Who can we help this week?
Today Isaiah 58:8–9
“Then shall thy light break forth as the morning … thou shalt cry, and he shall say, Here I am.”
When we fast and serve, the Lord promises to answer. Where have you felt Him say “Here I am”?
Today Isaiah 59:16, 20
“And the Redeemer shall come to Zion”
Jesus is the Intercessor who steps in when we can’t save ourselves. Thank Him for one way He has helped you.
Today Isaiah 60:1
“Arise, shine; for thy light is come”
Jesus and His gospel are a light that helps us find our way home. How can you shine it for someone today?
Today Isaiah 61:1–3
“to give unto them beauty for ashes, the oil of joy for mourning”
Jesus read these words in Nazareth to say He is the Messiah (Luke 4:16–21). How has He given you beauty for ashes?
Today Isaiah 65:17–18
“be ye glad and rejoice for ever in that which I create”
When the Savior comes again there will be peace and joy. What can we do today to get ready for Him?
Today Isaiah 58:13–14
“call the sabbath a delight”
What would make today more of a delight for our family? Share one idea at dinner.
What would make Sunday more of “a delight” for us? 58:13–14
